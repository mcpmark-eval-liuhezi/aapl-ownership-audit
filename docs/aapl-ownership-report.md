**What this means for the vote:**

- Apple is overwhelmingly institutionally held — roughly two-thirds of the company sits with 7,707 institutional reporters. The free float is extremely liquid, which is consistent with the stock's tight spreads and the heavy index-fund representation below.
- Insider ownership is thin at ~1.65%, typical for a mega-cap with broad-based equity compensation. It is not a founder-controlled company; there is no insider block large enough to sway outcomes at the margin.
- Insider stake + institutional stake ≈ 68%, leaving roughly a third of shares with retail and other non-reporting holders.

### 5.2 What stands out

- **Timothy Cook's 191,753-share sale for ~$63.85 million on 2026-10-02 is by far the largest recent insider disposition.** It follows a 374,541-share option exercise the prior day, so it reads as a routine exercise-and-sell under a 10b5-5 plan rather than an ad-hoc exit — but it is the number the club will hear about. He also gifted 26,325 shares the same day.
- **Arthur Levinson's 250,000-share sale (~$71.19M) on 2026-05-06 was the largest single-value transaction in the set**, paired with a 65,000-share gift and a further 50,000-share sale ($15.55M) later in May.
- **CEO John Ternus sold 25,412 shares (~$8.46M) on 2026-10-02**; COO Sabih Khan exercised 99,878 shares on 2026-10-01 with no matching sale on the list.
- General Counsel Jennifer Newstead shows a steady program of small, near-weekly sales of ~1,400–3,000 shares through the year — the classic pattern of an automated selling schedule.
- **The direction of travel is one-way: sales and gifts only.** No insider purchases appear in the returned data. That is normal for Apple, where compensation is overwhelmingly equity-based, but it means the tape offers no insider buying signal to lean on.
- Every sale in the recent window occurred between roughly $248 and $347 — the stock has roughly tripled off lows in this data's history, so insiders are realizing gains rather than abandoning a position.

## 6. Summary for the Vote

1. **Ownership base:** 66.35% institutional across 7,707 reporting institutions; 1.65% insider. Extremely liquid, institutionally dominated register with no controlling insider block.
2. **Biggest institutional holder:** BlackRock at 7.97% (1.16 billion shares, ≈ $404.4B), and it increased its position +1.6% quarter-over-quarter.
3. **Biggest fund holder:** Vanguard Total Stock Market Index Fund at 3.19% (466.3 million shares, ≈ $162.1B).
4. **Insider signal:** All sales and gifts, no purchases. Largest: Levinson $71.19M (May), Cook $63.85M (October). Most activity is consistent with scheduled, option-linked selling rather than opportunistic exits.
5. **Valuation context:** the stock trades at 39.9× trailing earnings, near its 52-week/all-time high of $351.73, up 29.4% over 12 months versus 10.9% for the S&P 500 — the ownership picture itself is stable, but the entry price is the live question for the club.
